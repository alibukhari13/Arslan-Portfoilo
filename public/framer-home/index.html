FAQ

We’ve heard it all. Here’s everything you need to know before working with us.

What do I need to get started?

You only need a clear idea of your product, website, or problem you want to solve. I handle research, structure, and design direction so you do not need everything figured out upfront.

What kind of projects do you usually work on?

I work on websites, landing pages, dashboards, and product interfaces. Most projects involve ui ux design, design systems, and scalable layouts built in figma and framer.

How involved will i be during the design process?

You are involved at key decision points. I share progress regularly, explain design choices clearly, and collect feedback early so the final outcome aligns with your goals.

What tools do you use for ui design?

I primarily use figma for ui ux design, design systems, and structured layouts. For advanced projects, I use framer to present the UI with interactions and motion.

What will i receive after the project is completed?

You will receive clean figma files with structured components, auto layouts, and design systems if applicable. For small projects, you may also receive a framer link showcasing the UI.
